## Short technical defense

- **Why templates before a live agent?** They isolate data, retrieval, policy and review failures at low cost. Live reasoning has not demonstrated incremental value yet.
- **Why no classifier?** Synthetic labels/current features do not support a defensible held-out predictive claim. A readiness gate blocks training.
- **What does +19 points mean?** A conditional diagnostic difference on 100 previously seen authored tasks, including expected abstentions; 20-family descriptive bootstrap, not business lift.
- **What did you learn from failure?** Evidence can be authentic but irrelevant. Candidate conflicts/instruction checks hurt coverage; answerability needs independent evaluation.
- **How would you measure ROI?** Randomized account assignments, human prep/review/rework and completion/quality guardrails, mature outcomes and actual costs. API milliseconds cannot substitute for human time.
- **What is not production-ready?** Live Google enrollment, runtime-role/RLS/restore/retention/load validation, crash recovery and recommendation quality. No external delivery is available.

## Rehearsal and fallback

Build frontend assets, start PostgreSQL and the local synthetic helper with a fresh operator-supplied password. Confirm `/health/ready`, executive login, known source lookup and reviewer inspection. Use distinct browser contexts. On a stale approval/session or DB outage, show the explicit failure/reference, reauthenticate/refresh as appropriate, and never blindly repeat a review decision.

If local services are unavailable, use the desktop/mobile/approval screenshots and `RevenueOS-Comparison-Attempts.jsonl` as clearly labeled **recorded evidence**, not a live demo. Keep the failed gate visible. Finish with the architecture and one measured limitation instead of claiming all production gates passed.
